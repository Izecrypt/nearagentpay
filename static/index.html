NEAR Hackathon · agentic payments

nearagentpay

Give an AI agent a task and a NEAR budget. It plans priced steps, routes each to the cheapest live provider quote, and settles every step on-chain through a facilitator account. Every transaction is final, explorer-verifiable, and written to an audit ledger. Money moves when work moves.
